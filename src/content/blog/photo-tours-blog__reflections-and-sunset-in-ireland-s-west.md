# I'm not boasting but _Ireland is surely one of the best countries to live in_. It's scenic, there is no doubt, it's compact and easy to get around.

## There is nowhere better to be at sunset than the west of Ireland, the mountains and valleys soak up the low rays of sunshine coming from the Atlantic.  

And when you're done the pubs are calling, or bed is calling if you've been up all day to wait for sunset and intend to be up early tomorrow for sunrise!

Is it bad to say that being an Irish photographer is a good thing?

I don't think so.

The west of Ireland is only as scenic as many other places in Europe and elsewhere, but it still has the Irish factor that has been drawing visitors to our shores for thousands of years and causing melancholy among those who have left the .

Photographing in Ireland is pretty perfect; the misty landscapes, soft green hills and varied coastal landscapes - you have to try it for yourself.

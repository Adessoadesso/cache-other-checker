The west of Ireland - it has great people, great food, great entertainment and great scenery and that all makes for the perfect location for photography.

Here I arrived late in the day to find this flooded landscape with a quickly setting sun blocked by clouds before it dipped below the horizon.

I opted not to use filters in this particular image, preferring to capture the essence of the bright evening scene as it was.

Connemara is a region, the western part of County Galway, that makes for _perfect photography_. Factor in the stormy, fast-changing weather and there is a perfect combination for the **landscape** **photographer**.

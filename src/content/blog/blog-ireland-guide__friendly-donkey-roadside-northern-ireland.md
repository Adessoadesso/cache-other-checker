# It's no secret that Ireland is a scenic place, very often visitors are drawn to the big open landscapes, the famous places such as the **[Cliffs of Moher](/photo-tours-blog/cliffs-of-moher-county-clare-wild-atlantic-way-ireland-majestic-landscape-photography)**, **[Dingle](/photo-tours-blog/join-dingle-photography-tour-winter-summer-spring-autumn-kerry-wild-atlantic-way)** or the **[Giant's Causeway](/photo-tours-blog/unesco-giants-causeway-antrim-coast-photography-course-workshop)**.

## But there are many fine places to be found in other parts of the **Emerald Isle**.

### On my travels around the back roads of Ireland, I was looking for an old graveyard beside a river.

As I was stopped by the roadside a friendly farmer on his way past told me that he had many donkeys in his field around the corner, to go and photograph them and that's what I did.

As I walked along the laneway, this friendly donkey came up to the gate followed by his companions and seemed happy to pose for a few pictures.

It's not the first time of course that I have photographed donkeys in Northern Ireland, **[here's one](/miscellaneous-blog-category/i-often-get-asked-where-my-twitter-background-image-is-from)** from the mountainous area in the south of **County Armagh**.

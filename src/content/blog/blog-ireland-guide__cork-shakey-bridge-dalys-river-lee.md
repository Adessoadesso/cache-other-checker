This is a quick walkthrough, exploring the famous River Lee bridge opened in 1927 to connect the north and south banks of the city in place of a ferry.

Daly's Bridge got its nickname of the Shakey Bridge due to its tendency to shake as people walk across it.

The repairs will preserve the shaking feature of the bridge so locals should be happy.

I photographed the suspension bridge and its striking engineered structure in early November, complete with beautiful reflections in the River Lee below.

Check out the video of the walk across the Shakey Bridge on YouTube.

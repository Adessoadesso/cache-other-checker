### Coastal Landscape Photography Workshop February

The coast of Ireland is well known and justly so with long, golden sandy beaches, rugged, indented and rocky coastline with castles, cottages and mountains perched precariously looking out over the sea.

From the sheer cliffs of Slieve League in County Donegal to the world famous Cliffs of Moher in County Clare and the Giant's Causeway in County Antrim, there can be no doubt that the coast of Ireland is perfect for photography.

Panoramic Ireland run monthly workshops on various parts of the Irish coast. February 2015 is the County Antrim coast in Northern Ireland, image is from this workshop.

For more information about Panoramic Ireland's coastal landscape photography workshops click [here](/contact "Antrim Coast Photography Workshops by Panoramic Ireland - that's me!") or visit this link: [http://panoramicireland.com/photography-workshops-ireland/coastal-photography-course](/photography-workshops-ireland/coastal-photography-course) The price per person for a weekend is €300 and you will need filters to make the most of the two days. I can give you advice on filters and any other equipment.

The March 2015 workshop will be held in County Galway. To learn more and register your interest please send me an email via the [Contact](/contact) page.

# Ireland is a green country, except when it [snows](/blog-ireland-guide/snow-ireland-country-roads-march-2018-storm-emma-beast-from-the-east-snowmageddon "Snow in Ireland - Country Roads") and even in [winter](/blog-ireland-guide/snow-ireland-december-2019-mountains "Ireland in Winter, Green and White") (February in the image above) that green still shows in the fields and woodlands of the Irish countryside.

## Here, in a fine little woodland of beech, ash and oak a cascading stream flows over rocks as the low rays of the winter sun brightens riparian scene of mosses, ferns and fallen leaves.

Photography in Ireland isn't just a summer interest, the country has many beautiful scenes to capture throughout the year.

# The sun sets on another day, another solar year passes, the days get longer from now on - Ireland and the northern hemisphere pass the the winter solstice.

## In Ireland the winter solstice has long been an important point of the year, Ireland's most famous monument at Newgrange is aligned with sunrise on the winter solstice (December 21/22) - read more **[here](/photo-tours-blog/newgrange-ireland-winter-solstice "Newgrange and the winter solstice")**.

### Reflected in the western beach Ireland's Wild Atlantic Way greets the colourful sunset, beaming from sheeny sands.

As is often the case, rain held off until the colourful part of the evening was finished.

Choice of where to photograph for the evening was important, and correct; based on forecasts and local conditions.

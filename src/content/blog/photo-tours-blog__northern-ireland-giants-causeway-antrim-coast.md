The Antrim Coast of Northern Ireland, home to the well-known Giant's Causeway is one of my favourite places to photograph, it is also one of the best places in which to lead a photography workshop.

Here, an image taken from a recent workshop that I led centred on the UNESCO World Heritage Site.

To find out more about Panoramic Ireland's photography tours and workshops in Northern Ireland see this page: [http://panoramicireland.com/photography-workshops-ireland/northern-ireland-photography-workshops-photo-tours](/photography-workshops-ireland/northern-ireland-photography-workshops-photo-tours)

The 40,000 basalt columns sit right on the coast and provide plenty of subject matter for the photographer and those wanting to learn how to improve their photographic skills.

With the wild North Atlantic washing over the lower columns, most of which are regular sided as seen in this image, there is often a lot of action or motion to be captured in the image.

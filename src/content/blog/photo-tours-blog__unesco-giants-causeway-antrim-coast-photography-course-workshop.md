# Join me on a **photography day course** at **Northern Ireland**'s fine **Giant's Causeway, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.**

## Learn more about when to use various settings on your camera - **Av** / **Tv** or **S** / **M** and how to choose which settings given the conditions.

### Learn how to use filters, specifically for coastal landscapes and how to plan for the weather.

#### The schedule is varied depending on the season, weather on the day and due to other factors but we will organise the day to suit each of these elements.

Price is £400 for the full day on a private one-to-one (or you and a friend), during September, October, November, December, January, February and March the day can run from sunrise to sunset, during the longer days of summer we will prioritise sunrise or sunset, or cover both while taking a break during the midday hours.

To learn more about Panoramic Ireland's photography courses, tuition and tours use the contact page or book a photography experience via this page **[/photography-workshops-ireland/northern-ireland-photography-workshops-photo-tours/book-a-photo-tour-in-northern-ireland-causeway-coast](/photography-workshops-ireland/northern-ireland-photography-workshops-photo-tours/book-a-photo-tour-in-northern-ireland-causeway-coast "Book a Giant's Causeway Photography Course")** - a supplement will apply to make it into this full day, balance due before the course begins or you can stick with the shorter session that is currently bookable direct through the site.

This course is extendable to cover post-processing including Lightroom and Photoshop as a separate session the following day to work on images taken during the day.

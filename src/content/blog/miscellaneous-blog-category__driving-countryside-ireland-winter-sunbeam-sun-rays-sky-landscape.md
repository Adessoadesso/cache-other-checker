# A fine afternoon sky from today in Ireland with rays of the sun . 

## Sunbeams break through fast moving clouds as the sun edges past the hypotenuse of this nebulous triangle into clear blue sky for a short time.

### The hills and low mountains of Ireland, illuminated in places into the distance.

#### After a fine scene of [golden winter light](/photo-tours-blog/winter-sunshine-irish-mountains-golden-hour-colour "Golden Hour Winter Sunlight in Ireland") yesterday, this definitely made a perfect afternoon outing with camera to mark three years since my cancer operation. Indeed it reminded me of [this image](/miscellaneous-blog-category/2019-a-difficult-year-cancer-diagnosis-chemotherapy-camera-photography "Cancer, Chemotherapy and Camera") from the time of my chemotherapy.

There is still good time left this winter for cold, snowy weather but so far 2022 has been mild. In a few days the 1st of February marks the traditional start of spring in Ireland with [Saint Brigid's Day](/blog-ireland-guide/february-1st-saint-brigids-day-first-day-spring-imbolc "1st of February - Start of Spring in Ireland").

Now that the various lockdowns and restrictions have ended here in Ireland, and hopefully we won't see more need for them, I will be photographing throughout Ireland all year and you can join me, Panoramic Ireland, in any part of the Irish countryside that you would like to visit.

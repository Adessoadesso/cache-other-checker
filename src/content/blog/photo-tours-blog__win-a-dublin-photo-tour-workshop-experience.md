# Win a one-on-one photography experience in Dublin

Choose from a street option to improve your urban photography or go to the coast and create better seascapes.

There are three ways to enter, send me a message through the [contact](/component/contact/contact/9?Itemid=466) page on the site, visit my Facebook page at [www.facebook.com/IrishImagesByPanoramicIreland.com](https://www.facebook.com/IrishImagesByPanoramicIreland.com) and message the page or simply Like one of the images in the post regarding the competition. I could of course give you a direct link to the post, but I'm sure that having used the link to get to the page you can find the competition post! Any troubles just let me know, it sounds more difficult than it actually is.

So if you are based in Ireland or just visiting then my photography experiences are perfect for you; part-tour and part-workshop we will cover the basics of photography or find you the best locations.

And why not enter the competition to win a Dublin photography experience with Panoramic Ireland? I can't think of any reason not to - it's free, it's good fun and 

The competition closes at 23:59 Irish time on 29/05, the winner can arrange a photography experience with Panoramic Ireland just like we usually do. Dates are flexible to suit your schedule and we can start as early or as late in the day as you want.

# The day was long and we visited the coast at sunrise, followed by a foray into the woods of Ireland's interior.

We made the coast our main focus but how could we have resisted the colourful charms of this woodland scene? Would you pass on this one?

Here we looked down onto the small but fast-flowing stream from a little stone bridge before following the stream for a mile or so.

Landscape photography in Ireland is a fun and often challenging adventure, join me on a photography workshop in Ireland's woodlands.

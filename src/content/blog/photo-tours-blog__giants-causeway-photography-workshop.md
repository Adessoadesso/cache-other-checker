# A photography evening on the Giant's Causeway in Northern Ireland.

I'm from the area originally and used to cycle out to the Causeway when studying for my Bsc Geography in nearby Coleraine.

And now I photograph the Causeway Coast regularly, leading photography tours and workshops to the UNESCO World Heritage Site and the other famous sights.

On this occasion I met with Terry and Terry in from the USA and we photographed in a few locations before heading to the unique hexagonal columns that have drawn tourists to this part of Ireland for centuries.

We covered classic angles and long exposures, using ND filters.

Read more about Terry and Terry's travel adventures [**here**](http://terryandterryblog.com/the-antrim-coast-of-northern-ireland/ "Terry and Terry's Blog").

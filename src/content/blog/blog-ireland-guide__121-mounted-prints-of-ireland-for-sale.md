We will be selling some of our favourite prints at the [Christchurch Cathedral Market](http://www.christchurchdublin.ie/News---Events/Christ-Church-Craft-Market.htm "Panoramic Ireland Selling Prints at Christchurch Cathedral Market") in Dublin this weekend.

The market runs from 1200-1700 on Bank Holiday Monday the 6th of August.

Feel free to ask about our photography tours and workshops and learn how to improve your photography in just 4 hours.

We sell only our own, original, mounted, framed and plain photographic prints of Ireland and we can ship Worldwide if you don't want to carry a print around with you on the rest of your holiday.

All images are printed on archival quality photo paper, with archival quality mount board and mounted prints can be shipped in Ireland, Britain, the EU, USA, Australia and beyond.

Mounted print prices begin at €10 and for a limited time only 12x8 mounted prints are available for €20 plus postage.

A new online print sales shop will be ready soon but in the meantime if you see any image on this website [Contact Us](/contact "Contact Panoramic Ireland to buy Prints of Ireland") today to enquire about sizes and postage rates.

# Street photography can through up some unusual situations and give rise to interesting photographs.

## Here, a visiting bishop arrives, Saint Patrick maybe, with his travel case on the cobbled alleys of Dublin and stops to talk with, or perhaps berate, the street vendors with their sandwich boards advertising Thai massages in Dublin.

Street photography usually involves the skill of watching and anticipating situations, what's happening and how will it look for instance - positioning yourself in relation to the subjects and being ready to photograph at the **decisive moment**.

Join **Panoramic Ireland** on a **photography workshop** in **Dublin** to cover the basics of street photography.

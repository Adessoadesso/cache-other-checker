I have been spending time photographing and leading **photography workshops** recently in **Dingle**, Ireland's most westerly peninsula in County Kerry.

Here is an image of the **Atlantic**, its waves crashing up over ancient rocks under a stormy sky.

This is a beautiful part of Ireland and I have written about leading photography tours in [**Dingle**](/photo-tours-blog/photography-workshops-dingle-co-kerry-ireland) before on the blog and how, aside from keeping the camera steady for landscapes in windy weather, the toughest aspect of a visit to Dingle is how to choose a favourite pub.

This week I have one slot available for my private Dingle **one-to-one photo tours** on _Tuesday 8th August 2017_, so if you are looking a unique way to see **Ireland**'s southwest then use the **[booking](/photography-workshops-ireland/book-a-dingle-ireland-photo-tour "Book a Panoramic Ireland Dingle Photography Tour & Workshop")** form or contact me to get more information.

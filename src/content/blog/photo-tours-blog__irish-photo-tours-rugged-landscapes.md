The rugged mountains of Ireland stand over the flat boglands of waterlogged earth.

Here the peaks, not particularly high, rounded by erosion during the Ice Age stand guard over the Irish landscape as they have for aeons.

The blue sky and warm weather brings white clouds floating high above, not a sound to be heard except for the sound of three camera shutters and wild birds in this open landscape.

Join me to photograph places in Ireland like these.

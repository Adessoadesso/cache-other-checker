Autumn is almost over but this week Ireland has been treated to a riot of colour as fine, clear weather brought blue skies and golden light to highlight the myriad colours of the autumnal Irish countryside.

On a photography workshop with AH from Washington DC, we found plenty of colour and scenery to keep our cameras occupied, sometimes I wonder if the sensor can keep up with all of the colour!

It can of course but what a sight to see.

Winter will soon be here and our photographic adventures here at Panoramic Ireland will soon change to different locations so it's time to make the most of this colour now.

Join Panoramic Ireland, that's me, on a photography tour in Ireland this winter and photograph the Irish countryside.

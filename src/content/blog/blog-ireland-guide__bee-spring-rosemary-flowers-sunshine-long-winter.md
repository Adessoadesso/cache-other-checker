# Bumblebee _Bombus_ enjoying fresh rosemary flowers _Rosmarinus officianalis_ on a warm spring day in Ireland.

## I wrote in my [last post](/blog-ireland-guide/2018-spring-arrives-warm-temperatures-sunny-blue-sky-april) yesterday that the long cold winter days are behind us as warm temperatures have finally arrived in mid-April 2018.

The bees, of many types, visited flowers throughout the long, sunny day and I captured some on camera as they went about their busy buzzing.

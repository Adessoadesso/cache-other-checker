# It has been a sad old year, 2019. News broke in 2018 that Google Plus, the much derided social network, would close in April 2019.

## January also saw me diagnosed with cancer, have a tumour removed and in March receive chemotherapy.

### Both of these things have it seems coincided.

I have enjoyed using Google Plus over the years, with almost 200,000 followers on my famous **Ireland** collection and hundreds of +1s and comments per post, Google Plus became a place where it was possible to reach and chat with an audience who had a deep interest in Ireland, in my case. Different to those typically found on other social media networks, and sadly misunderstood by those who decided to shut it down.

Here, an image of a small waterfall at the entrance to a cave covered with greenery that seems peaceful and relaxing.

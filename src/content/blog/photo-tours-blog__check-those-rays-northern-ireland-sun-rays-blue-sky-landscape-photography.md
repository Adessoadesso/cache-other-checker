# After a long day of photography in the Irish countryside, nothing can be better than watching and photographing the sunset.

## I have of course written about sunsets here on Panoramic Ireland before, here is a link to some **[sunset images](/miscellaneous-blog-category/2019-a-difficult-year-cancer-diagnosis-chemotherapy-camera-photography "Sunsets from a chemotherapy, cancer patient")** from when I was undergoing chemotherapy and recovery from a cancer operation this year, sunset was easy to photograph.

As we travelled along the backroads, the sky looked ever more promising so instead of heading to our intended destination directly, the sensible thing was to head towards a viewpoint to photograph this majestic scene of sun rays and blue sky, created with the help of that big cloud to scatter and concentrate the sunlight in different directions at the same time.

Then on to our destination for the final sunset image but for me this was the image of the day, a finely balanced nature inspired photograph of the beauty of the Irish landscape.

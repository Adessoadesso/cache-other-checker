# Every so often a little bit of colour catches your eye, here by the coast of Ireland I noticed every breaking wave and its spray carrying a rainbow of colour towards the white cliffs of the Antrim Coast.

## Rainbows are usually fleeting, relying on a mixture of sunshine and water vapour meeting at the correct angle to create the separation of light into its constituent wavelengths - for us we see the visible spectrum of red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo and violet.

### Rainbows can occur anywhere, as seen here in the wave spray or on top of a mountain in a [ringed glory](/blog-ireland-guide/croagh-patrick-pilgrimage-brocken-spectre-ringed-glory-irish-mountain "Brocken Spectre and Ringed Glory on Croagh Patrick, Ireland"), in [urban settings](/photo-tours-blog/rainbow-portobello-dublin "Rainbow in Portobello, Dublin") and in fine [autumnal scenes](/photo-tours-blog/rainbow-river-ancient-monastic-glendalough-wicklow-weather-monastic-city "Autumn Rainbow, Glendalough").

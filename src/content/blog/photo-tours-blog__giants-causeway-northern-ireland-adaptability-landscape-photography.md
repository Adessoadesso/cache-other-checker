# Northern Ireland's **UNESCO** World Heritage Site - the **Giant's Causeway** on the Antrim Coast.

## It's a fine place, perfect for exploring your skills and vision as a photographer. 

### As with any landscape photography  the weather often adds drama or puts obstacles in the way and that is all part of the charm of being out in the open.

Adaptability is one of the key skills for any photographer, landscape photography is no different.

Join me here at the Giant's Causeway to learn how to make better images and use your camera to its, and your, full potential, find out more here - [/photo-tours-blog/unesco-giants-causeway-antrim-coast-photography-course-workshop](/photo-tours-blog/unesco-giants-causeway-antrim-coast-photography-course-workshop)

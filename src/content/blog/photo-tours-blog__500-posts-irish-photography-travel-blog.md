This post marks **Panoramic Ireland**'s 500th blog post here on panoramicirelanc.com.

A year after treatment for cancer and I am still posting of **photographic adventures** throughout **Ireland**.

The image above is of **[Cork](/photography-workshops-ireland/book-a-cork-ireland-photo-tour "Photography Tours in Cork by Panoramic Ireland")**, the River Lee at night and **Saint Finbarre's Cathedral**. I photographed this fine scene for **Eater**, the world's most popular food website as part of their [Where to eat in 2020](https://www.eater.com/2020/1/7/20961410/best-places-to-eat-travel-2020) and Cork was one of the few cities in Europe to be on the list. Have a look at the images and Cork article **[here](https://www.eater.com/maps/best-restaurants-cork-ireland "Eater - Cork the city's best restaurants for 2020")**.

500 posts; what a time it has been, photographing in every county in Ireland (that's all 32 if you don't know). Sometimes at sunset, sometimes at sunrise and often in the middle of the day.

From Dublin to Galway to Belfast to Cork and all points in between, the Irish Sea coast and the Wild Atlantic Way.

Here's to the next 500 posts, send me an email to say hello and drop a comment into the box below.

Come and join me on a photographic tour in Ireland. for instance in **[Cork](/photography-workshops-ireland/book-a-cork-ireland-photo-tour "Cork Photography Tours, Ireland")**.

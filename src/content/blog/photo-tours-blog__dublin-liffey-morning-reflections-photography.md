# Morning Reflections from the River Liffey, Dublin

## I usually photograph the River Liffey at night with the city's iconic buildings reflected in its calm waters.

Here is one from the morning, an early morning just after sunrise, of that low sunlight shining on to the quayside buildings.

On our way to photograph more of Dublin we stopped here to photograph this colourful scene of navy ships moored alongside the glassy architecture of the city's docklands.

Panoramic Ireland's photography tours and workshops in Dublin take place every day of the week, all year round.

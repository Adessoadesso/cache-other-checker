# Peace descended over the west of Ireland at the end of yet another fine January day.

## There was no direct sunset, instead a bank of cloud moved in and the sun slipped in and out of the grey band stretched across the sky, crepuscular rays emanating in many directions for 30 seconds or so at a time.

### Ducks quacked, [geese](/blog-ireland-guide/geese-ireland-green-greylag-goose-migration-nature-wildlife-anser-anser "Greylag Geese, Ireland") honked as the countryside began to darken before the long night, the time after sunset that we call the [blue hour](/blog-ireland-guide/early-morning-dublin-swimming-forty-40-foot-blue-hour "Blue Hour Morning Swim, Ireland").

### After many storms Ireland is experiencing an extended calm with respite from the incessant rain and wind of the last week of December which saw several storms including [Gerrit](/blog-ireland-guide/moody-incoming-storm-gerrit-december-2023-ireland-winds-rain-flooding-storm-henk-on-the-way "Storm Gerrit and Storm Henk visit Ireland"), Pia, Geraldine and Henk.

It's been a busy few weeks here for me in Ireland, and with Earth Day I wanted to post an image that shows some of the beauty of the world around us.

Here in this small urban park cherry blossoms are doing their bit to make the city cheery with as much colour and flair as possible. This is not a natural landscape, but so little of Ireland has no influence from humankind yet it is apt for Earth Day, demonstrating the possibilities when humans and nature work together.

This is one of my favourite images and it has been used in calendars by companies and UCD (University College Dublin).

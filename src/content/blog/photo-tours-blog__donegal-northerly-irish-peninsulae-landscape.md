Donegal is Ireland's most northerly county, its peninsulae jutting out into the North Atlantic with Malin Head being the northernmost point on the island.

And it is a scenic part of Ireland, the coast is long with rocky sections, **[cliffs](/photo-tours-blog/slieve-league-sliabh-liag-donegal-cliffs-atlantic "Slieve League Cliffs, Donegal, Ireland")**, sandy beaches and with more than a few **[lighthouses](/photo-tours-blog/scenic-fanad-lighthouse-donegal-atlantic-ireland-most-photographed "Fanad Lighthouse, Donegal, Ireland")** it lives up to its reputation as a rugged place.

The Wild Atlantic Way really does begin in Donegal.

# Vouchers for Photo Tours in Dublin & Ireland

## We often get asked if we can provide vouchers for photography tours and experiences, and the answer is always **yes**. To buy a voucher please send us an email enquiry through the website at the contact page. Currently it is possible to buy specific dates for Dublin Photo Tours through the secure booking system that accepts **PayPal** by the way! 

### The link to buy a **Dublin Photo Tour** is **[here](/photography-workshops-ireland/book-a-photo-tour-in-dublin "Book a Dublin Photo Tour")**, for the **Antrim Coast** and **Causeway Coast** of Northern Ireland click **[here](/photography-workshops-ireland/northern-ireland-photography-workshops-photo-tours/book-a-photo-tour-in-northern-ireland-causeway-coast "Book a Giant's Causeway Photography Workshop")**. More direct bookings can be found under the Photo Workshops section of the menu above.  
  
All vouchers entitle the holder to the _same photography experience_ as buying direct- there is no difference and come with a twelve month expiry date so there is a lot of time to book a day of photography - just pick your favourite season!

All that we suggest is that the holder contacts us as soon as possible to enquire about and make a booking that way they can get the most out of the voucher. Vouchers make brilliant Christmas or birthday gifts for friends and family who are interested in photography and are suitable for all levels, from beginner to advanced photographer and are suitable for locals and visitors to Ireland alike.  
  
We also offer vouchers for our residential Galway photo courses, to buy please use the form on our [**contact**](/contact "Contact") page. Find out more about our **Galway** photography workshops [here](/photo-tours-blog/panoramicireland-are-now-running-residential-photography-tours-and-workshops-in-galway-includes-sample-itinerary "Photography Workshops in Galway, Ireland").  
  
All vouchers are non-refundable and have no alternative cash value but you can transfer them to a friend and we're flexible with organising a day of photography for you or your friend. Early booking is advised to get suitable dates. Vouchers are available year round as birthday presents or just for someone visiting Ireland, not just at **Christmas**.

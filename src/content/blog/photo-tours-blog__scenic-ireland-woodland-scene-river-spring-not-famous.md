I do enjoy visiting the famous places in Ireland, there's **[Kilkenny](/photography-workshops-ireland/book-a-kilkenny-ireland-photo-tour "Book a Kilkenny Tour")**, **[Dingle](/photography-workshops-ireland/book-a-dingle-ireland-photo-tour "Book a Dingle Tour")**, [**Dublin**](/dublin "Find out more about Dublin Photo Tours") and the **[Giant's Causeway](/photography-workshops-ireland/northern-ireland-photography-workshops-photo-tours/book-a-photo-tour-in-northern-ireland-causeway-coast "Book a Giant's Causeway Tour")** to name a few.

But I also enjoy leading photography workshops in the non-famous places here in Ireland - those places that make up the bulk of the Irish countryside such as here in the above image of a typical woodland scene in spring.

Here the start of the longer days and warmer temperatures has brought the green foliage that covers the Irish countryside, the small river flowing as it has done for thousands of years through the peaceful and secluded valley.

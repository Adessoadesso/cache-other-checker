# I often get asked about how the weather is in Ireland in winter.

## Well, it's hard to say anything definitive but in general, Ireland is green all year round including in winter.

### Just like in the image above from the west of Ireland in December.

Typically a winter daytime temperature will be around 5C but colder conditions and [snow](/photo-tours-blog/sunset-winter-snowy-landscape-ireland-mountains) sometimes occur. The mild winter temperature is what allows the grass to keep its healthy green colour, blanketing the countryside.

And of course it is often [sunny in winter](/blog-ireland-guide/winter-photography-ireland-no-snow-lots-of-colour), so although it might not be as warm as you are used to, the weather isn't usually extreme.

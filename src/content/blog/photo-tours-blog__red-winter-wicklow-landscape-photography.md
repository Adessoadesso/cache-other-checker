# Ireland's Wicklow Mountains are scenic throughout the year, in any season.

## Seen here in winter with a little snow in the distance, the colour red dominates the wild landscape under a blue sky. Although [Ireland is mostly green](/photo-tours-blog/wicklow-mountains-lough-tay-panorama-irish-landscape "Wicklow Panorama - Green Landscape of Wicklow"), even through the winter, there are places that are less so.

### If you are looking for something to do close to Dublin when you are in Ireland, take **[Panoramic Ireland's Wicklow Photography Workshop](/photography-workshops-ireland/book-a-wicklow-ireland-photo-tour "Wicklow Photography Workshops")** to see and photograph this stupendous county.

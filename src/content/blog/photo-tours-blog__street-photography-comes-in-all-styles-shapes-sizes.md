## Street photography comes in all styles, shapes and sizes.

## Here during the current snow storm to hit Ireland, known as **Storm Emma** and the **Beast from the East**, a man shielded by an umbrella walks up the steep, snow-covered Dawson Street in Armagh, Ireland's ecclesiastic capital.

Choosing to walk by preference on the road, rather than on the footpath up the steep hill, one of the small city's seven hills - just like Rome.

Dawson Street climbs up to the Church of Ireland cathedral, one of two dedicated to Saint Patrick in **Armagh** who founded his principal church here in 445 AD.

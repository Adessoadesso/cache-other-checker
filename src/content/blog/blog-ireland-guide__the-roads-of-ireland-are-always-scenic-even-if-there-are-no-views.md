##### Backroads in Ireland

It can hardly be called a large country, Ireland. It's possible to drive from the **Irish Sea** to the **Atlantic** in around **three hours** but the backroads of Ireland are some of the most scenic it is possible to encounter.

From the narrow stone-lined laneways of the Aran Islands to the wooded valley roads of Northern Ireland there is always something scenic to look at even if the views are obscured by mist and trees. 

Enjoy the atmosphere, stop and take a photo. The journey is worth the effort and the views might not be of great wide valleys or lofty mountains, but they are views nonetheless.

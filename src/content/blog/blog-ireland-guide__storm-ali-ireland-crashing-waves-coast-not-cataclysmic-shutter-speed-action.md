# Storm Ali raced across Ireland last week and Panoramic Ireland was photographing around the coast of **Ireland**.

Not really to photograph the storm but leading a photo tour to photograph the coast, the waves and the Irish Sea. It definitely wasn't cataclysmic.

And we weren't disappointed, we found calm bays and rocky coast with breaking waves, plenty of sunshine and a good opportunity to cover photographing action using various shutter speeds.

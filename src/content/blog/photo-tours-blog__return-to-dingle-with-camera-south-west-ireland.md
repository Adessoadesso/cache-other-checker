As I mentioned in previous posts, this week saw me returning to **Dingle** in Ireland's southwest.

And I wasn't disappointed, neither of course was JM who joined me from the USA for one of my **one-to-one photography workshops** in **Ireland**.

His choice on this occasion, after having braved the rain and stunning sunset of **[Dublin](/dublin "Dublin Photo Tours")** a few days ago, was Dingle - a stunning part of Ireland with coast and mountains and green fields.

Here as we waited on the sunset is some of that magical light that we encountered on the 6 hour photography workshop in this most westerly part of Europe - green fields, the roadway and distant mountains.

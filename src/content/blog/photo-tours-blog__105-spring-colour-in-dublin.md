I have been asked by many photographers recently if there is much colour around in Ireland at the moment.

![Colour in Springtime Ireland](/images/blogWM/untitled-1wm.jpg) 

Prior to arriving for a photography workshop it is only natural that a photographer is interested in what there is to see and photograph.

Well, it's Spring here in Ireland. The snowdrops have been and gone, daffodils are here and everywhere cherry blossom is adding a touch of colour to the hedges of Dublin and Wicklow and the parks of the city centre. Of course the landscape is green, and red and yellow in places - it's always colourful!

Think of spring, think of colour. Think of cherry blossom and Dublin doesn't spring to mind.

From my previous post on [cherry blossom in Dublin](/photo-tours-blog/dublin-herbert-park-cherry-blossom-spring-colour) I have had many comments expressing surprise that Dublin looks quite so colourful, that looking at the image of bright pink blossom overhanging paths it looks more to the eye like Japan or other countries associated with the beautiful flowers.

Here is another from one of Dublin's fine parks with blossom and fresh green foliage on a bright spring day.

I do enjoy spending time in Dublin and exploring its many parks and you can join me on one of my [Dublin Photography Tours](/dublin).

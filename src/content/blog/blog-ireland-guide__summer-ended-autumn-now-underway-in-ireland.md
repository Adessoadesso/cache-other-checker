Summer has finished in Ireland although a late burst of fine weather in late September is helping to make up for what was a disappointing few months; it was summer only in name.

Autumn has begun and we have the beginning of a many coloured landscape.

Here the trees are lined up at the field's edge ready to perform, showing their best colours, for those who have time to appreciate the beauty of the countryside.

As a photographer of landscapes this is an exciting time of year and the autumnal scenes to be encountered over the next few weeks in Ireland are likely to be worth every effort made to photograph them.

You will be unsurprised to learn that trees and leaves will make up a great proportion of the images taken during September, October and November.

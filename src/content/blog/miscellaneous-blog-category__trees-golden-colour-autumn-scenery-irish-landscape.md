# Bright golden autumn colour on one of Ireland's many back roads.

## While some Irish thoroughfares get all the attention, [The Dark Hedges](/blog-ireland-guide/dark-hedges-bregagh-road-antrim-northern-ireland-scenic-avenue-game-of-thrones "The Dark Hedges - a Leafily Scenic Avenue") and [Grafton Street](/blog-ireland-guide/bewleys-coffee-shop-grafton-street-dublin-reopens-2020 "Grafton Street - Bewley's") being two prime examples, the country is accessible from a dense network of small back roads that, in many places, are incredibly scenic.

### Just colour here beside a small river, enough to make you want to stop and just take it all in for the few weeks of autumn every year.

#### The season is changing now and after lots of windy but warm weather recently most of the leaves are off the trees. The bare season of winter is nearly here.

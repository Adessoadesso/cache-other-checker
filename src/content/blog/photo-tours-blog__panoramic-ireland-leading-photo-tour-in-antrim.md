# Antrim Photography Experience

I wrote about a recent photography workshop on the Antrim Coast with two Sicilians, see [here](/photo-tours-blog/photography-experience-on-the-antrim-coast-northern-ireland) - we went along the coast, photographing waterfalls, green fields, rocky coastline and of course the Dark Hedges. This is real landscape photography territory.

Here, we are setting up to photograph a waterfall in a wet and lush valley. Long exposure was the order of the day and tripods were necessary, especially because the day was overcast and the valley was covered in vegetation.

Panoramic Ireland run photography tours and workshops in Antrim and throughout Ireland.

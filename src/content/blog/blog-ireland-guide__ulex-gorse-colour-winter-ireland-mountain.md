# Winter can often be described as a season without colour and lacking in sunshine, but it isn't usually the case at all in Ireland.

## Recent weeks have seen plenty of sunshine and snow in the mountains, see some of my last posts for these [here](/photo-tours-blog/more-snow-ireland-february-2018 "`Snow in Ireland, February 2018").

Today I headed into the mountains, camera in hand as always, I found some of the most colourful plants to be found in the Irish countryside - **gorse** or _Ulex europaeus_ also known as furze or whin.

_Aiteann gallda_ is its Irish name and I have written about this characterful plant of the Irish landscape in my book **Portrait of Armagh**.

So make a visit to Ireland in winter and expect to see green fields, blue skies and yellow flowers of gorse, join me for a tour in Ulster, Munster, Leinster and Connacht.

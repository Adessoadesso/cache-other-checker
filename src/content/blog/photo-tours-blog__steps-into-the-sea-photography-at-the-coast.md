_Steps into the sea_, my favourite subject matter is the coast and, **Ireland,** being a small island makes access to the coast easy. 

Here the natural shapes of the coast, smooth rocks and the blue of the sea are split by man-made elements - the steps and rusted metal railings cutting through the scene yet even these are being slowly reclaimed by nature.

**Panoramic Ireland**, _that's me_, create photography workshops on the coast of Ireland from the **Causeway Coast** to the **Wild Atlantic Way**.

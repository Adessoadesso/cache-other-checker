Dublin's iconic **Custom House** at night, reflected in the River Liffey.

I posted a photo of the famous Dublin landmark as it was bathed in bright golden sunlight, see here: [/photo-tours-blog/dublin-beautiful-colour-sunset-river-liffey-custom-house](/photo-tours-blog/dublin-beautiful-colour-sunset-river-liffey-custom-house "Dublin's Custom House at Sunset") but I have photographed this building more often at night over the past twenty years.

Designed by architect **James Gandon** in the 1790s the Custom House now functions as a scenic focal point on Dublin's River Liffey.

Often it will be suggested that the real name, somehow, is the Customs House - with an 's' however, street signs, maps, the government department based in the **Custom House** and [archiseek](http://archiseek.com/2010/1791-custom-house-customhouse-quay-dublin/ "Dublin Custom House") all refer to it as the latter.

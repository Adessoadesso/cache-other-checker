# Dublin's Docklands underwent significant, almost complete redevelopment in the 2000s with the biggest names in the tech industry still building and locating in the area today.

## Here in Grand Canal Square the public realm was designed by landscape architect [Martha Schwartz](https://msp.world/projects/grand-canal "Martha Schwartz Grand Canal Square, Dublin") and the choice of red paving blocks and these tall red glow sticks were designed deliberately to give a red carpet effect coming from [Daniel Libeskind](https://libeskind.com/work/bord-gais-energy-theatre-and-grand-canal-commercial-development/ "Grand Canal, Bord Gais Theatre by Daniel Libeskind")'s 2,000-seater theatre.

### On the right is the distinctive chequered pattern of the [Anantara The Marker Hotel](https://booking.tp.st/s6cTd45P "Anantara The Marker Hotel").

#### This is now a good-looking part of the city and the redevelopment has brought a lot of life into the area that was run down for many decades at the end of the 20th century.

A return to County **Cork** took me to it's beautifully varied **coastline** that I have previously mentioned [here](/blog-ireland-guide/ireland-coast-cork-wild-atlantic-way-cloudy-weather "Cloudy Day at the coast, Co. Cork").

Unlike on that previous occasion the weather was not cloudy and moody but **bright** and **colourful** highlighting the bright **greens** found in the littoral landscape.

Actually this was my second time in Cork this week, on Wednesday 29th of June I had a media pass to photograph **Don Henley** and his support act **JD & The Straight Shot** at Cork's famous Marquee venue. There is a short post about that [here](http://darrenmcloughlin.com/jd-and-the-straight-shot-cork-ireland-2016 "JD & The Straight Shot supporting Don Henley").

Here, just after leading a **photography tour**, I stopped at this small cove where the lighting ranged from bright to overcast. The tide was out so it wasn't a suitable location during the tour itself when we visited more interesting sections of the coast.

But I found it **worthwhile** to stop for a break after a long day and enjoy the quiet calm offered by the inaccessible portion of the coast particularly as the tide was coming back in and providing some interest.

I captured a few of these scenes looking out to sea and nearby of agricultural landscapes, then after a long day it was time for fish and chips, taking in the best of the **Wild Atlantic Way**'s cuisine.

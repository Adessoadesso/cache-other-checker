# For over 10 years now **Panoramic Ireland**'s famous [**Dublin Photo Tours**](/dublin "Panoramic Ireland's Dublin Photo Tours") have been taking locals and visitors alike around Dublin's famous and not so famous sights.

## Yesterday was no exception with RM in from Austin, Texas. We headed off in the afternoon to **explore** some of the city's interesting places.

### The weather was fine, and it was my first full tour after having had chemotherapy for cancer last week so I wasn't sure what to expect from my own ability.

But it turns out to have been a very good afternoon's sightseeing in the city, covering several miles.

As we explored the leafy avenues and graffiti-covered lanes in **Dublin** we went through some of the **goals of photography** - capturing and recording your travels, creating artworks and telling a story; as well as covering some of the technical aspects such as choice of aperture, keeping an appropriate shutter speed and use of white balance settings.

Photography is often a personal pursuit, and that is why Panoramic Ireland's **private one-on-one photography classes** / tours / workshops and small group holidays are perfect for the photographer who wants to learn more about their **camera** or **personal vision** as an artist.

[**Contact**](/contact) **Panoramic Ireland**, that's me by the way, to find out more about **photography tours** and training in Ireland.

The first video is a little 15 second overview of the sunset in three stages.

And the full video, speeded up by 8x using Garmin's Virb Edit software to can be seen on the Panoramic Ireland YouTube channel. At first, clouds race across the sky in two directions before clearing ahead of sunrise. Every so often a little breeze brings some ripples into the shallow bay as the sun traverses the clear blue sky. The hawthorn bush in the foreground catches the sunlight and the long grasses move with the breeze. The golden hour of beautiful clear sunlight finally gives way to a calming blue hour as the landscape darkens ahead of night.

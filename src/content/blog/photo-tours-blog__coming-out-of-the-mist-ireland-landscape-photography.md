Sometimes the weather doesn't give you what you want but it gives you something far far better.

This was the case on the occasion that I wanted to photograph the long landscapes of the Irish countryside.

Instead I was met with low cloud and swirling mist across the mountain slopes until, briefly, the cloud cleared.

I have written about this kind of weather before: [http://panoramicireland.com/photo-tours-blog/misty-mountain-ireland-trees-islands-favourite-image](/photo-tours-blog/misty-mountain-ireland-trees-islands-favourite-image)

Join me on a photographic adventure somewhere in the Emerald Isle to photograph scenes like these.

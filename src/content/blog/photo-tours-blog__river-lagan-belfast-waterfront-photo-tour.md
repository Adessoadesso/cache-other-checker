# **Belfast**'s **River Lagan** cuts a snaking path through the edge of the city, the true city centre situated a little to the west over the now covered Farset river from which Belfast gets its name.

## _Béal Feirste_ is the Irish name for the second largest city on the island of Ireland, meaning Mouth of the (River) Farset.

The industrial city has many fine sights, the river Lagan as seen here and close by is the shiny **[Titanic Belfast](/blog-ireland-guide/belfasts-titanic-quarter-shining-example-of-regeneration)** while the city centre has murals, pubs, street art and architecture to rival any other city and a **[unique history](/photo-tours-blog/the-peace-walls-of-belfast "Belfast's Peace Walls")**.

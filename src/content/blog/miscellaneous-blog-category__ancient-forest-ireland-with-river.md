### Ancient Irish Woodland

I have written about [ancient woodland in Ireland](/photo-tours-blog/116-ancient-woodland-co-kerry-ireland) woodland before. In certain parts of Ireland the remnants of ancient woodland survive, here is one such place nestled high in the mountains inaccessible from the demands of the wood cutter.

On a photography workshop in the mountains of Ireland this is one of my favourite places, it is not a famous place but it is a place as worthy of attention as anywhere else.

Here is an image of a stream, moss covered tree and swirling water in a hidden corner of the Irish landscape taken today - a day that for me, started at 05:00 and is still going at the time of this post.

# Bright and characterful sunset sunlight on Ireland's Atlantic Ocean.

## This image comes from the western edge of the Emerald Isle as the late evening sunlight fades behind rain-bearing clouds to give dramatic lighting on the ocean's surface.

### As we waited for the same sunlight to strike the **Cliffs of Moher** we turned our cameras toward the west and were not disappointed.

The sun then shone back on to the immensely scenic western edge of Ireland's landmass, see here: **[/photo-tours-blog/cliffs-of-moher-county-clare-wild-atlantic-way-ireland-majestic-landscape-photography](/photo-tours-blog/cliffs-of-moher-county-clare-wild-atlantic-way-ireland-majestic-landscape-photography "Sunlight on Ireland's Majestic Cliffs of Moher")**

As you can see it was a beautiful evening and one which was markedly different from a previous visit to the Cliffs of Moher: **[/photo-tours-blog/sunset-photography-experience-cliffs-of-moher-ireland](/photo-tours-blog/sunset-photography-experience-cliffs-of-moher-ireland "Cliffs of Moher at Sunset")**

That's the fun of photography in Ireland, you never know what the weather will give but it will always be interesting and quite likely challenging.

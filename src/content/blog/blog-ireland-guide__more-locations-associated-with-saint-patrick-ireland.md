I have written about Northern Ireland's locations associated with Saint Patrick in [this](/photo-tours-blog/northern-irelands-st-patricks-locations "Northern Ireland's Saint Patrick Locations") post and since it is Ireland's national saint's day once again, that's the 17th March for those of you who don't know, why not show off some more locations with links to the snake-banishing, shamrock-wielding Welshman who became a hit in his adopted country Ireland.

And where better to include, other than the spectacular Croagh Patrick mentioned [here](/blog-ireland-guide/croagh-patrick-pilgrimage-2013 "Croagh Patrick pilgrimage, Ireland"), Dublin's Saint Patrick's cathedral, a well in Tipperary, and Cashel also known as Patrick's Rock in Irish.

All beautiful places I'm sure you will agree.

Happy Saint Patrick's Day 2017 and I look forward to seeing you in Ireland soon!

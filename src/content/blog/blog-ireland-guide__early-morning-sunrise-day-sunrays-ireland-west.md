A recent photography tour in the west of Ireland encompassed several sunrises but of these this was the most impressive.

As I stood in front of a typical Irish landscape of green fields bounded by high stone walls of rounded limestone I watched as the early morning rays of sunlight broke through the heavy cloud to illustrate the west of Ireland landscape.

A quiet scene unfolded although I had places to be .

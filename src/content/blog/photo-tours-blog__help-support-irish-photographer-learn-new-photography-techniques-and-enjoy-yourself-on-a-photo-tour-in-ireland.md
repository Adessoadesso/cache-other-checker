### Photo Tours in Dublin and Ireland

It's fun and exciting to lead photography tours in Dublin, meeting everyone from professional photographers to those who just enjoy taking photos.

**Everyone has the ability to create a good image**, and that is the basis of our photo experience in Dublin or your chosen county in Ireland.

We want you to learn something new, find somewhere you wouldn't have seen and enjoy yourself on our photo tours.

Our photo tours are suitable for all levels and we run them on a one-to-one basis, or in small private groups (for you and your friends). If there are a few of you, just split the cost between you.

If you want to add some post-processing work, using **Adobe Lightroom** and **Photoshop** then we can also arrange that - starting with capturing the image at the best time of day to organising, editing and preparing your own images for print or uploading to your website and social media sites. The digital darkroom aspect of photography is very important and what you learn here will add to the overall look of your online presence as a blogger, writer or photographer.

We rely on your support here at PanoramicIreland.com and we want to craft your own photo tour in Ireland.

Panoramic Ireland's Dublin photo tours have been listed as one of the **top five things to do in Dublin** by the Travel section of the **New Zealand Herald**.

Sitting alongside Dublin's most popular tourist attractions in the guise of The Guinness Storehouse, The Book of Kells in the Long Room Library at Trinity College and the tourist mecca of Temple Bar, our photography tours were commended for their **tailored**, **one-on-one** basis.

Forget the group tours, if you want to take a photo tour of Dublin that is tailored to your interests - choose from street photography / nightscapes / architecture / seascapes /pubs or a mix of them all, take the whole thing at your own pace and extend it to a full day if you want.

We are currently offering private guided photo tours in Dublin, lasting approximately 4-5 hours, for **€215** for one to four people. So bring a friend or three for some fun and to share the cost, for dedicated photography tuition and an insider's eye on the capital of Dublin; or if you are on your own let's make it a dedicated photography workshop. Check availability and book a Dublin Photo Tour [here](/photography-workshops-ireland/book-a-photo-tour-in-dublin "Book a Photo Tour in Dublin") and remember all photo tours or workshops include image critique and advice on using Photoshop, Lightroom and managing your images.

If you have a look through the blog section of the site (you are currently in it) you can read more about a 'typical' photo tour (actually there is no such thing on a Panoramic Ireland Photo Tour!), and you can find more information about our Dublin tours [here](/dublin "Dublin Photo Tours"). If you have any questions though, please feel free to ask.

For those family members who are not so keen on photography there is plenty of interest with a lot of history and beautiful cityscapes to talk about. With a degree in Geography, a lifetime of travelling and photographing throughout Ireland and practical and research experience in Irish archeaology there are a lot of subjects to discuss. And we can even help you to take better images with your compact camera, camera phone, film camera or whatever you have with you.

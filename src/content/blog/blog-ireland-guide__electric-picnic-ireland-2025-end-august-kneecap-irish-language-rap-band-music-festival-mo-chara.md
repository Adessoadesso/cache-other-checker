## It was without doubt that the Belfast Irish-language rap trio Kneecap would be the biggest single draw to the Main Stage at Electric Picnic 2025.

Performing middle afternoon, Kneecap were the real headliners on a rainy/sunny/cloudy day in the Irish countryside with their energetic and irreverent performance before the vast majority of the festival's 80,000 attendees.

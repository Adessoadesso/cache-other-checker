Autumn, is there a more colourful time of year?

Here on a photography workshop we set out to find colourful woodlands and streams, here we found the perfect combination of both.

Flowing water comes past a full spectrum of colourful, autumnal leaves recently changed from lush green to yellows, reds, browns and still the moss green remains on the tree trunks illuminated by low sunlight.

Join me on a photography workshop in the beautiful Irish countryside all year round, even December and January.

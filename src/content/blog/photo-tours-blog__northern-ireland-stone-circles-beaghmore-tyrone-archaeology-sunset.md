# There are currently plenty of challenges with visiting Ireland, the country is now the most expensive in Europe for the cost of living, hotels are charging [astronomical rates](https://www.irishexaminer.com/news/arid-40896530.html "Hotel Rates in Ireland Damaging Reputation") and the recent [car hire challenges](https://www.rte.ie/news/ireland/2022/0523/1300743-car-hire-costs/ "Car Hire Damaging Tourism in Ireland") are being reported almost daily in the international press.

## And of course that's assuming you can get here, flights are relatively cheap but with many [cancelled flights](https://www.rte.ie/news/business/2022/0627/1307032-aerlingus-ryanair-flight-disruptions/ "Cancelled Flights to Ireland") in and out of Ireland and [huge delays at Dublin Airport](https://www.euronews.com/travel/2022/05/31/hellish-queues-outside-dublin-airport-in-the-early-hours-as-passengers-try-not-to-miss-fli "Queue at Dublin Airport Stretches Mile") the difficulties are real.

### While all of the above is true, there can be no doubt that Ireland is one of the most beautiful countries to see and to photograph.

### Join me, **Panoramic Ireland**, to photograph in the Irish countryside such as here at these fine stone circles - Beaghmore, Co. Tyrone, one of my **favourite places to photograph**.

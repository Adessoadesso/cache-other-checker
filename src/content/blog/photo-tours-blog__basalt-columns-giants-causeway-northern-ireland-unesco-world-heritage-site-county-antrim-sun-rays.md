Northern Ireland's Giant's Causeway is a place that I have photographed often.

Read more about the history, mythology and geology of the unique coastal geographic feature in one of my previous posts **[here](/blog-ireland-guide/photography-northern-ireland-unesco-giants-causeway-geology "Northern Ireland's Giant's Causeway")** - it's an interesting read.

I have just returned from a visit to the famous tourist attraction and I found this image from a previous visit where the sky was thick with clouds but the sun did its best to break through, showing some bright sun rays in the distance over the Atlantic and County Donegal.

My most recent visit has been a different experience, we photographed late into the evening but with bright blue skies and low sunlight.

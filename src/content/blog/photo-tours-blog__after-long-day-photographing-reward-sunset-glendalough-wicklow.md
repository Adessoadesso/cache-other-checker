# Sunset at **Glendalough** after a long day of **photography in Ireland**

### We spent from before dawn right through to sunset photographing in Ireland's County Wicklow, situated just south of Dublin it is an area that I know very well.

The journey after lunch was long and we headed towards County Wicklow's most visited place - **Glendalough**.

Situated in a valley of two lakes (Glendalough actually means literally _valley of two lakes_), the scenic place made for a perfect location to head to for sunset and we were not disappointed.

The sun was low and we set up our tripods to photograph the valley scene but it was after [sunset](/photo-tours-blog/west-of-ireland-photography-workshops-sunset-landscape-photography "Sunset in the West of Ireland") when the whole landscape was bathed in **pinky-orange light** that we photographed the reflections, as seen in the image above - and it was just beautiful to stand and watch the beauty of nature in front of us.

It was simply a perfect end to a **great day of photography**.

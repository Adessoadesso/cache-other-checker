# I have written about Connemara here on panoramicireland.com many times before, including **[here](/photo-tours-blog/connemara-ireland-land-of-bog-mountains-sea-and-sky "Bog, mountain and sky in Connemara")**.

It's a place that I know well, a place that I have photographed many times. Often I have stood in the rain, capturing the misty scenes of this part of Ireland, many times I have captured fine sunsets and explored the [region's beauty](/photo-tours-blog/west-of-ireland-landscape-mountains-sky-blue-panorama "Mountain scenes of Connemara").

Here a scene of fast moving clouds and ruffled water zooms past a tree-laden island and barren mountainside, a riot of colour.

Below, another image of a reflection from one of the lakes of Connemara, immortalised in the French language by Michel Sardou and his song Les **Lacs du Connemara**.

Join me, Panoramic Ireland, to photograph Connemara, the Antrim Coast, Dublin or Wicklow. Actually if you want to learn how to improve your photography anywhere in Ireland, then panoramicireland.com is for you.

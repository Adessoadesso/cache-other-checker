On a recent landscape photography workshop in Ireland's west we encountered a beautifully bright and colourful sunset at the coast of County Mayo.

After a long day on the road, two photographers met with the golden light and bright colours that stream across the Atlantic late on a summer's evening.

Alone except for a surfer and her dog we spent over an hour photographing the beach and sky before heading back to where we were staying.

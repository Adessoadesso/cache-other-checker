There is nothing quite like an evening at the coast on a calm evening with a colourful sky and still waters reflecting all above.

There are many days in the year when this is not possible, days when of course images of the power of the sea are exciting to photograph - days like **[this](/photo-tours-blog/storms-2014-raging-sea-wild-atlantic-way-connemara-ireland "Stormy Atlantic Ocean, West Coast of Ireland")** for instance. But there is no doubt that a colourful image like this is both evocative and emotive.

This was taken on the Irish Sea coast after sunset during a photography workshop.

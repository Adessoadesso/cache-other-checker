A visit to the coast of Ireland is a pleasure to be enjoyed at all times of the year, good weather and bad.

Here, an early morning shot taken between long exposures and panoramic images. 

Whatever way I look at it, this is my favourite from the day. The feeling of solitude, with nothing but the sound of gently breaking waves, oystercatchers on the rocky shore below me at times and the click of the camera shutter made for a peaceful moment away from the hustle and bustle of everyday life.

The destination doesn't have to be exotic, famous or even just well-known. It just has to be like this.

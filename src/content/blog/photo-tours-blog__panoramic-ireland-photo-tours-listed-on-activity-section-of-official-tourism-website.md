Panoramic Ireland’s photo tours have been listed on Discover Northern Ireland’s website in the activity section complete with some information on what Panoramic Ireland offers in photo tours and workshops throughout Northern Ireland.

The information is specific to Northern Ireland where I have created photo tours in Antrim, Down, Fermanagh and Tyrone. Strangely, despite Derry City being the City of Culture 2013 I have not yet led a tour there, any takers?

For the rest of Ireland the process is exactly the same be it Belfast, Dublin, the Antrim coast or Connemara.

Recent tours have taken me to [Belfast](/photo-tours-blog/recent-photo-tour-in-belfast-and-north-down-coast) and North Down, Newcastle and [South Down](/photo-tours-blog/panoramic-image-of-the-mourne-mountains-in-northern-ireland), the Antrim Coast, South Armagh and Fermanagh. All of these areas contain stunning landscapes and locations that are of interest to the visitor and local alike- from the mural covered walls of Belfast to waterfalls and woods in the Northern Irish countryside.

There has never been a better time to visit Northern Ireland, it has always been a stunning part of Ireland and now with much progress made in the Peace Process, the development of the tourist industry and the friendliness of the people it is even better. And what better way to see Northern Ireland than on a photo tour - find more information [here](/photography-workshops-ireland/northern-ireland-photography-workshops-photo-tours).

To find Panoramic Ireland on Discover Northern Ireland - the Official Visitor Website for Northern Ireland - click [here](http://www.discovernorthernireland.com/Panoramic-Ireland-P38656) and don't forget to say hello, enquire or ask for advice on Northern Ireland or photography.

# **Autumn** has arrived this week with the passing on the 22nd of September of the Equinox and the arrival of cold weather, minus temperatures and frosts overnight.

## The Irish landscape is still green though, and the fresh weather is bringing quite a bit of blue sky.

Here, a [green road](/blog-ireland-guide/green-road-ireland-scenic-quiet-photography-adventure "Green Roads in Ireland") or country lane meanders through the rolling green hills of the Irish countryside.

Ireland has plenty of famous avenues, the Dark Hedges being one, but those without hordes of people are those that I prefer.

Join me, **Panoramic Ireland**, for a photographic adventure in Ireland - from the [coast](/photography-workshops-ireland/coastal-photography-course "Coastal Landscape Photography Workshops in Ireland") to the [mountains](/photo-tours-blog/west-of-ireland-is-perfect-for-photography "Connemara - Mountains").

# Today's image comes from the South Bull Wall in Dublin Port.

![Arriving into Dublin, Ireland](/images/blogWM/_3241832-edit.jpg)

During the early evening several ferries arrive from the UK, Stena from Holyhead, P&O from Liverpool and Irish Ferries Ulysses (pictured here) from Holyhead.

It was a nice evening for a walk out into Dublin Bay, on the wall begun in the eighteenth-century, with plenty of activity on the sea from boats, geese and cormorants.

More information on ferries arriving into and out of Ireland can be found at my guide to [Getting to Ireland](/component/content/article/return-to-irelands-river-shannon-at-sunset-with-camera?Itemid=603&catid=8 "Getting to Ireland by Sea") by Sea.

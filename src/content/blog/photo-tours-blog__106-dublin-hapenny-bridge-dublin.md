After a recent photo tour I spent a bit of time photographing around the Ha'penny Bridge across the Liffey in Dublin.

![The Liffey and Ha'penny Bridge in Dublin, Ireland](/images/blogWM/halfpenny_bridge_tryptichwm.jpg)

 These have a little bit of green in them, an appropriate subject for today - St. Patrick's Day. It's the day that everyone wants to be Irish.

The weather looks to be mixed, a nice dry and sunny start to St. Patrick's Day but rain will spread from the west during the afternoon. St. Patrick's Day for the past few years has been a fairly grey and wet occasion. Unfortunately because the weather in March is often very nice, it can be warm and sunny.

# It's almost Christmas, the end of another year and another decade; here at **Panoramic Ireland** we have been busy **photographing Dublin** as always.

## 2019 has reached December and, for the second year, more than a dozen of **Dublin**'s buildings and streets have been illuminated to give the city a more welcoming feel.

### Created by Dublin City Council, **Winter Lights Dublin 2019** adds a charm to the city in the dark days of Winter.

And without doubt these have been successful, the city's **Millennium Bridge** for instance has become a **[selfie hotspot](/blog-ireland-guide/dublin-winter-lights-2019 "Dublin's newest selfie hotspot, Millennium Bridge")**.

Enjoy the city, if you are in Dublin over Christmas do have a look for the lights and why not book a **photo tour** with me, Panoramic Ireland, to see the best Dublin has to offer and learn how to make better images.

# Metal music legends **Judas Priest** brought the **Invincible Shield** to Dublin, Ireland a few days before Saint Patrick's Day.

## I was there to photograph Rob Halford, Richie Faulkner and the band, indeed you can see some of my images on their [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/OfficialJudasPriest/posts/974354494035968 "Judas Priest Facebook - Dublin Invincible Shield 2024") and [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/C4ltkBLyi4F/?img_index=1 "Judas Priest in Dublin, March 2024 Invincible Shield Tour").

The rest below in the gallery.

No need for a **Panic Attack**, if you can get to some select European and US cities in 2024 then you'll know that **You've Got Another Thing Coming**.

Enjoy the sound of fifty years of metal!

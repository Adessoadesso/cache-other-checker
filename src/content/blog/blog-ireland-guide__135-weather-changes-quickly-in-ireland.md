The weather changes so quick here in Ireland that I always recommend keeping an eye on the weather forecast - it gets more accurate the closer you get. It's hard to trust anything more than 3 days ahead.

  
  
![Changeable Weather in Ireland](/images/blogWM/20130614_170652.jpg)  
  
Here we have the difference from one June evening to the following morning, a big change. One day can be warm and the next cold so always carry a rain jacket and maybe some extra layers in case it gets chilly.  
  
Of course the highly changeable nature of the Irish weather makes for some of the most [dramatic photographs](https://fbcdn-sphotos-g-a.akamaihd.net/hphotos-ak-frc1/576254_10151264206490470_1636487447_n.jpg "Changeable weather makes the best images").

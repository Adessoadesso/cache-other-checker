# No landscape photographer's spring catalogue would be complete without images of bluebells _hyacinthoides non-scripta_ carpeting a woodland floor.

## Here on our photography workshop that also dealt with photographing in inclement weather, as we **[photographed streams](/photo-tours-blog/swirling-water-woodland-stream-ireland-landscape-photography)** and forests so too did we concentrate on the colour in front of us.

While they didn't carpet every square inch of the beech woodland floor, they still looked incredible at the end of May amongst the lush green grass and ferns of the Irish countryside. And that path, encouraging us further into the woods.

Bluebells are the classic spring time woodland photographic subject, the greens are fresh in most forests by May and the blue sits against the green vividly. At the other side of summer, the autumn colours of leaf fall is the classic **[woodland scene](/photo-tours-blog/woodland-stream-autumn-ireland-reflections "Autumn woodland river scene, Ireland")**.

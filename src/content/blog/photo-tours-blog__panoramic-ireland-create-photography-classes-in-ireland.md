## Panoramic Ireland - Photography Classes in Ireland

This year I am running several photography classes around the coast of Ireland, from Antrim to Dingle these classes are suitable for all levels from beginner to advanced and all you need is a camera. It doesn't matter what type of camera you have, there is no snobbery here.

I would say, however, that a camera that allows you to take long exposures via a bulb timer would be great as it will enable us to create some long exposure images like that featured here.

I can provide the locations and indeed I have been photographing the coast of Ireland for many years, some more of my coastal images can be seen [here](/component/k2/tag/coast). 

Coastal photography classes start from €185 or £185 and you can enquire by contacting me via the [contact page](/component/contact/contact/9?Itemid=466).

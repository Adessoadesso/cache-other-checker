We might be restricted right now from moving too far from home but if you have a garden, balcony or window box then and some flowers then the chances are you have bees visiting.

Here the white-tailed bumblebee _Bombus lucorum_ sits on rosemary flowers on a sunny day. I wrote about bumblebees back in February in regards to the traditional start of Irish Spring, **[Saint Brigid's Day](/blog-ireland-guide/february-1st-saint-brigids-day-first-day-spring-imbolc)**.

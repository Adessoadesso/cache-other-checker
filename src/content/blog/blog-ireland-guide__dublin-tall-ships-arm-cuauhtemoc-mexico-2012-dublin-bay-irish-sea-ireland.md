## The ARM **Cuauhtémoc**, one of the world's finest tall ships seen here in Dublin Bay 2012.

Under sail this is one of the most impressive of the touring training ships, seen here with dozens of cadets working the rigging high on the ship's masts as it leaves the safety of Dublin's River Liffey for the Irish Sea as the [tall ships leave Dublin](/blog-ireland-guide/124-tall-ships-2012-leave-dublin-sailing-past-howth-into-irish-sea "Tall Ships Leave Dublin").

The **Cuauhtémoc** has made headlines recently due to the bridge strike on the Brooklyn Bridge in New York where unfortunately two of its crew died and its masts were damaged.

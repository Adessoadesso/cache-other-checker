**Dublin's parks** are getting more colourful by the day, the cold weather has arrived and it does feel like the summer is really over.

Horse-chestnuts _Aesculus hippocastanum_ are the first trees to turn yellow and lose their leaves, with others such as the lime trees pictured here following suit. 

Like in any city, urban parks make a great location for photography at this time of year and it seems that each park has its own character.

Can you think of anywhere better for a stroll, or a photography experience?

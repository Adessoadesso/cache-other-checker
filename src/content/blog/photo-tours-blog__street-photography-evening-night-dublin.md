# **Dublin** is a fine city of wide avenues and cobbled streets lined with pubs.

## Famed for its nightlife the world over, _Dublin has more pubs than any other county or city in Ireland_.

### With approximately **750 pubs**, that figure was a lot more in the past, Dublin's pubs are often cited by tourists and visitors to the city as one of the key reasons to visit Ireland.

Here, people enjoying an evening out in Temple Bar stand outside the most photographed pub in Ireland, The Temple Bar. 

I converted this image to black and white to suit the subject matter, but this pub is more recognisable by its bright red paint.

As a point of interest, we are soon coming up to Easter and in years gone by pubs were closed in Ireland on Good Friday but now, it is definite - **[Irish pubs are open on Good Friday](/blog-ireland-guide/irish-pubs-now-open-good-friday)**.

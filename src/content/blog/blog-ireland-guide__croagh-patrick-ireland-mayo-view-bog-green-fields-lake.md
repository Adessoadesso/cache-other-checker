# I have written about Croagh Patrick many times here on Panoramic Ireland, most recently with the story of a **[brocken spectre panorama](/blog-ireland-guide/croagh-patrick-pilgrimage-brocken-spectre-ringed-glory-irish-mountain "Brocken Spectre on Croagh Patrick")**.

And here is a view from the other side of this majestic peak situated out on the Atlantic Coast of County Mayo in Ireland's west.

Looking inland over bogland, forest, lakes and mountains the Irish landscape looks colourful in the sunset light glow under a cloudy, textured west of Ireland sky.

It's a scene of virtual emptiness, just one farm is visible in this long landscape.
